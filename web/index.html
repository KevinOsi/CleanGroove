CleanGroove

Remove clicks from a vinyl recording, right in your browser. Your audio is processed on your own computer and never uploaded.

Drop a recording here, or click to choose WAV or FLAC works best (MP3 and others play, but lossy files smear clicks). A few minutes is plenty for a demo.
